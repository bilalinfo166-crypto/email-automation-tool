How WarmWire works

Every email automatically carries your company footer + a working unsubscribe. Nothing sends until your profile is complete and you confirm you have permission to contact each business.

  1. Complete your company profile (once).
  2. Connect your sender inboxes (Gmail via Google).
  3. Upload the business domains you want to reach.
  4. Run the scraper to pull each site's public business email.
  5. Review contacts, create a campaign, confirm lawful basis, and send.
Make sure your Python backend is running (uvicorn app.main:app --reload) before using this page. Then click Connect top-right.
